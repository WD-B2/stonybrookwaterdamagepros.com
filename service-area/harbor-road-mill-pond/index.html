Where Harbor Road and the Mill Pond are.

Harbor Road runs along the harbor side of Stony Brook Village, near Main Street, and the Mill Pond, also called the Grist Mill Pond, lies beside the old Stony Brook Grist Mill. A grist mill has stood on the stream since 1699, and the current building dates to about 1751. News 12 reported that residents worried emergency vehicles lacked a direct route to the eastern half of the village after the washout.

We describe the area by these landmarks, because we did not find an official boundary map. If your address is nearby, call and tell us the street. The dispatcher can tell you whether we can reach you.

Before you go back into a wet or muddy space.

  • Stay out of standing water if the electrical panel, outlets or appliances might be live..
  • Treat floodwater and mud as contaminated and keep children and pets away..
  • Do not use ordinary fans or a household vacuum on contaminated water..
  • Wear boots, gloves and eye protection if you must enter, and leave the removal to professionals..
  • If you smell gas or see structural movement, leave and call 911 or the utility..

How cleanup differs when the water came from outside.

Water that enters from a pond, a road or the ground carries silt, debris and bacteria. Under the published S500 standard, that kind of water is treated as contaminated, which means porous materials such as carpet, pad and some insulation are usually removed instead of dried. Hard surfaces can be cleaned and dried.

Our flood damage restoration page walks through extraction, removal, cleaning and drying in order. For rain that comes in through a roof or window, see storm water damage cleanup.

Dryness takes longer than it looks.

A basement or ground floor can look dry while the framing and subfloor still hold moisture. Meters and thermal imaging find those pockets. Dehumidifiers and air movers then pull the moisture out until readings reach their drying goals. See structural drying and dehumidification.

If your property was affected in 2024 and still smells musty or shows stains, ask for a moisture survey. We will tell you plainly what is wet and what needs to come out. For basements, see basement water cleanup.

Flood insurance and your records.

Homeowners and flood policies are different.

The New York State Department of Financial Services advises that flood damage is generally covered only by flood insurance. Check your policy, file promptly, photograph damage before cleanup and keep an inventory and a log.

We keep photos, moisture readings and drying logs so the record is clear. We do not decide coverage and we do not promise any outcome from your insurer.

In November 2024, CBS New York reported that dozens of Suffolk homeowners said FEMA denied their requests for individual assistance, and that the Governor planned to appeal. We have not verified the final outcome, so do not assume federal help.
