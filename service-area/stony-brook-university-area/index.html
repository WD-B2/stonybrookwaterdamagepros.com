Safety steps in a wet rental.

  • Keep everyone out of rooms with standing water, a bulging ceiling or exposed wiring..
  • Do not turn on lights, outlets or appliances in or near the wet area..
  • If water came from a toilet or drain, treat it as contaminated and keep children and pets away..
  • If you smell gas or see sparks, leave the house and call 911 or the utility..

First steps for tenants and landlords.

  • Stop the water at the valve or appliance if it is safe..
  • Send the landlord or property manager a message with photos and keep a copy..
  • Move belongings out of the wet area and photograph them first..
  • Do not turn on electrical equipment in wet rooms..
  • Keep receipts for hotel stays, repairs and replacements..

Heavy use makes small leaks bigger.

When a house is shared by several people, kitchens, bathrooms and laundry rooms may run all day. A slow dishwasher or washer leak under heavy use can soak a subfloor before anyone notices. If you see a stain on a ceiling below a bathroom or a soft spot by an appliance, call early. Our appliance leak page explains what to check.

Carpet is common in rentals and holds water a long time. See carpet water damage restoration for how extraction, pad removal and drying work.

What the 2024 storm did and did not show.

FOX Weather and ABC7 reported that Stony Brook University relocated and sheltered hundreds of students and canceled move-in on August 19 and 20, 2024, after nearly 10 inches of rain fell in the area. ABC7 also listed state insurance help sessions at the University on August 27 and 28.

We did not find any source that ties a specific off-campus street to flooding in that storm, so we do not name one. Our Harbor Road and Mill Pond page covers the area where damage was documented.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified for this page. To check one address, search it on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, and ask the Town of Brookhaven Division of Building about local requirements.

Landlord and tenant rules, without guesswork.

The Town of Brookhaven requires a rental permit for rental housing. The University's off-campus housing site says it lists only properties with a valid permit and tells renters to confirm that with the town. In 2017 the Town and the University publicly asked landlords near campus to follow building and fire codes.

We do not give legal advice. For permit and code questions, contact the Town of Brookhaven Division of Building. For disputes with a landlord, speak to the University's off-campus housing office or a tenant advocate.

Records that help either side.

The New York State Department of Financial Services advises documenting damage before cleanup, filing claims promptly and keeping a log. Dated photos, moisture readings and receipts help a landlord claim and a renters claim alike.

We keep photos, moisture readings and drying logs for every job. We do not decide coverage, and we do not negotiate between tenants and landlords.
