How water gets into a basement.

Groundwater and cracks.

Heavy rain pushes water against foundation walls and up through floor joints and cracks.

Sump pump failure.

A pump that loses power or fails lets groundwater rise.

Plumbing and appliances.

A burst pipe, water heater or washer can flood the lowest level.

Drains and septic.

A backed-up drain or overloaded septic system can bring dirty water in.

Knowing the source matters. Clean water from a pipe is handled differently from groundwater, and both differ from sewage.

Basement flooding emergency: what to do first.

Stay safe first.

  • Do not enter if water is near the electrical panel or outlets. Call an electrician or the utility if you are unsure..
  • Turn off power only from a dry location..
  • Avoid skin contact if the water may be contaminated..
  • Photograph the water line on walls and the damage to belongings..
  • Many panels and furnaces sit in basements. If water is near them, leave and call 911 or your utility before calling us..

Pumping, then drying.

Deep water is pumped out first. Very rapid removal can stress foundation walls if the ground outside is still saturated, so crews often lower the level in steps. Once the water is gone, drywall, carpet and framing need drying, because they stay wet for days.

When a basement leak turns into mold.

Dark, enclosed spaces dry slowly. If walls and carpet stay damp, mold can follow. Our mold remediation page explains how mold is assessed under New York rules. Fast drying is the best way to avoid it.

Waterproofing questions we hear.

Property owners often ask whether we install waterproofing systems. We focus on water removal, drying and cleanup. Waterproofing, such as drain systems, sump pumps and exterior grading, is specialist work done by a waterproofing contractor. We can tell you where the water entered and what we saw, which helps when you choose a contractor.

Check your gutters and downspouts as well. Water that is discharged next to the foundation can end up in the basement. Grading that slopes toward the house has the same effect.
