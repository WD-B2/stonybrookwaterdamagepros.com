Frozen pipe emergency: the damage happens during the thaw.

Water expands as it turns to ice. The ice itself often blocks the flow, so a cracked pipe may not leak until temperatures rise and the blockage melts. That is why a house can seem fine at night and flood in the afternoon.

If you have a pipe that is frozen but not yet leaking, the most useful step is to know where the main shutoff is before the thaw begins.

If a pipe is frozen right now.

Careful steps for a frozen pipe.

  • Open the faucet served by the frozen line so water can flow as the ice melts..
  • Warm the area gently with moving air, such as a space heater kept well away from anything flammable. Never use an open flame..
  • Locate and test your main shutoff so you can close it quickly if the pipe cracks..
  • If you see frost on a pipe or a bulge in the line, call a plumber and keep the shutoff within reach..
  • If a leak is near the electrical panel or an appliance, do not touch it. Call 911 or your utility first if you smell gas or see sparks..

Why a quick cleanup matters in cold weather.

Wet materials in a cold house dry slowly, because cold air carries very little moisture. Drying equipment that raises the temperature in the affected space and removes humidity is often the difference between a clean finish and lingering dampness that supports mold.

If wet materials stay in place for days, we may recommend our mold remediation service after the moisture source is handled. We would rather catch it early than explain it later.

Preventing a repeat freeze.

  • Insulate exposed pipes in basements, garages, crawl spaces and exterior walls.
  • Seal gaps around pipes where cold air enters, such as rim joists and utility penetrations.
  • Keep the heat on at a moderate setting if you travel in winter.
  • Disconnect hoses and shut off outdoor spigot lines before the first hard freeze.
