Who we are.

Stony Brook Water Damage Pros serves Stony Brook and nearby North Shore communities from 97 Main Street, Stony Brook, NY 11790, in the Stony Brook Village Shopping Center. We handle the first hours of a water loss, the drying that follows and the damage assessment that guides repairs.

We are a small, local operation and we keep our claims plain. We do not publish invented reviews, awards or numbers. What we can show you is how we work, and that is what this page covers.

How we work.

  • A dispatcher answers the 24/7 line and helps you decide what to shut off and what to leave alone..
  • A crew stops the spread, removes standing water and checks hidden moisture with meters and thermal imaging..
  • We explain the scope and the cost factors before work starts, so you decide with real information..
  • We dry to measured readings and keep logs and photos in a form your insurer can read..

Standards we follow.

Water damage work follows published industry standards. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers water damage restoration and ANSI/IICRC S520 covers mold remediation. We use them as references for how a job should be evaluated and carried out. We do not claim firm-level certification beyond what we can show you, and you should ask any company to explain how its steps match the standard.

What we do not do.

  • We do not repair plumbing, roofs or septic systems. We coordinate with the licensed trades who do.
  • We do not perform mold assessments or clearance testing. New York requires separate licensed professionals for that work on larger areas.
  • We do not quote a price from a phone call. The same leak can be very different once we see it.
  • We do not make health claims about mold or water.

How pricing and scope are handled.

Water damage jobs are priced by scope, and scope depends on what we find. We walk the affected areas, measure moisture and note which materials can be dried and which have to come out. Then we explain the work in plain language, including the cost factors that apply, before we begin.

Emergency steps that protect the property, such as stopping the spread and removing standing water, often start first because delay makes the loss larger. Larger decisions, such as removing a section of flooring or opening a wall, are explained before they happen. You decide how to proceed.

Records and follow-up.

Every job leaves a paper trail you can use. Photographs show the starting condition. Moisture readings show how the building dried. Logs show equipment placement and days. If you file an insurance claim, those records help the conversation with your adjuster, and you stay in charge of how you work with your insurer.

If something looks wrong after the job, call the same line. Water damage can hide, and we would rather recheck than leave you guessing.

Honest limits on what you can expect.

We cannot promise an exact arrival time, because it depends on the call, the job and the hour. We cannot say in advance how many days of drying a building will need, because that depends on what got wet and how deeply. We cannot tell you whether your insurer will cover a loss, because that depends on your policy.

What we can promise is plain communication. We tell you what we see, what we recommend and why. If something is outside what we do, such as a plumbing repair or a licensed mold assessment, we say so and explain who handles it.
