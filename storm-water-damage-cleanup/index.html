How storms get water into a house.

From above.

Wind-driven rain gets under shingles, around flashing and through skylights, then soaks insulation and ceilings.

Through openings.

Windows, doors and wall penetrations leak when pressure and rain direction push water sideways.

From the ground.

Heavy rain raises water against foundations and can enter basements through cracks and floor joints.

Through drains.

Overloaded drains and septic systems can back up, which is a different category of water.

What happened in Stony Brook in August 2024.

On August 18 and 19, 2024, almost 10 inches of rain fell in parts of Suffolk County. In Stony Brook, Mill Pond overflowed or breached on August 19 and part of Harbor Road collapsed. Six houses were damaged, including cesspools, and Stony Brook University canceled move-in as about 150 students were displaced.

The Mill Pond dam and Harbor Road are the best-documented local damage from that storm. Our Harbor Road and Mill Pond page gives the dated, sourced facts and what we do for properties nearby.

That event is a useful reminder that one heavy storm can affect roads, ponds, septic systems and homes at once. A property owner may face several kinds of water in a single loss.

Storm water emergency: what to do in the first hours.

Steps for the first hours.

  • Stay out of flooded areas if there is any chance of electrical hazards. Turn off power only if you can reach the panel dry..
  • Photograph the damage, including exterior openings, before cleanup..
  • Move valuables to dry areas and lift furniture legs onto blocks if you can do it safely..
  • If floodwater came from outside or a drain, treat it as contaminated and avoid contact..
  • Downed lines, sparks or a gas smell mean leave and call 911 or your utility before calling us. Never wade into water that touches outlets..

Flood versus storm damage in New York insurance.

The New York Department of Financial Services notes that flood damage is generally covered only by flood insurance, not a standard homeowners policy. Wind-driven rain through a damaged roof or window may be treated differently. Check your policy, call your agent and ask how your coverage applies.

To learn about flood risk for a property, check the FEMA flood map and ask the Town of Brookhaven building department about local requirements.
