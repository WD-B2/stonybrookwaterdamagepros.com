Privacy Policy.

How we handle information when you visit this site or contact us.

Effective 2026-11-12. This policy applies to the website at stonybrookwaterdamagepros.com, operated by Stony Brook Water Damage Pros.

What we collect.

This site has no contact forms and no accounts. When you call or email us, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, address and a description of the problem. Your phone carrier and our phone system may also record call details such as the time and number.

How we use it.

We use the information to respond to your request, arrange service, prepare a scope and price and keep records of work performed. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services on this site.

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and may display a map from Google Maps. Those services receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when they load. Their own policies describe how they handle that information.

Cookies.

We do not set advertising cookies. Embedded third-party content may set its own cookies according to its policy.

Your choices.

You can ask us to correct or delete information you have shared with us by emailing the address below. You can also block third-party content in your browser settings.

Contact.

Questions about this policy: Stony Brook Water Damage Pros, 97 Main Street, Stony Brook, NY 11790, contact@stonybrookwaterdamagepros.com.
