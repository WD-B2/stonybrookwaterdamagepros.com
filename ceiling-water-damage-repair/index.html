Water in a ceiling is an emergency because the board can drop without warning. We take calls 24/7 and offer same-day response when available, including after hours. While you wait, move people out from under the stain and place a bucket under any drip.

A bulging ceiling is an emergency, and a safety problem first.

Water collects on the back of the drywall until the board gives way. A bulge, a dark stain or drips from a light fixture are warnings. A saturated ceiling can drop without notice, and the water that comes with it can be heavy.

What to do right now.

  • Move people and pets out of the room under the bulge..
  • Turn off power to the room at the breaker if water is near lights or outlets and you can reach the panel dry..
  • Place a bucket under active drips and move furniture away..
  • Do not poke or press on a bulge yourself. Call and let a crew release the water safely..
  • If water is dripping from a light fixture or fan, stay out of the room and call 911 or your utility if you see sparks or smell burning..

Where ceiling leaks come from.

  • Plumbing above, such as a bathroom supply line, drain or tub overflow.
  • Appliances on the floor above. See appliance leak damage.
  • Air handlers in an attic. See AC leak damage.
  • A failed roof or flashing, which has its own section below.

Roof leaks and the damage they cause inside.

When rain finds a way through shingles, flashing, a vent boot or a skylight, the first sign is usually a stain on an upper-floor ceiling. By that time, water has already traveled through the roof deck, the attic insulation and the ceiling drywall.

We deal with the interior water damage. We remove saturated insulation, open the ceiling where needed, dry the framing and plan the repair. A roofer must stop the leak at the source, so we coordinate timing. If the roof is still open, drying will not hold, and each rain adds new water.

Stains that grow after storms, brown rings with darker edges and musty attic air are common clues of a roof leak. If a storm is the cause, our storm water damage page explains the wider picture, including wind-driven rain and clogged gutters.

Temporary tarping is something a roofer or contractor arranges. Take photos of the roof and ceiling before any cover goes on, so your insurer can see how the water got in.

Stains, texture and paint after the repair.

A ceiling stain can bleed through paint if the board stays damp or the stain is not sealed. A true repair replaces damaged drywall, tapes and finishes the seams and matches the existing texture where possible. A patch painted over a wet board is likely to fail.

What affects the price of ceiling repair.

For the claim, photograph the ceiling, the room above and any roof damage before cleanup. The New York Department of Financial Services advises policyholders to report promptly and make only temporary repairs until the insurer inspects.
